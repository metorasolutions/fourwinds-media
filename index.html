Get in Touch What We Do

Local radio isn’t disappearing. It’s misunderstood.

Up-close and personal communication has been lost to online interaction. 4Winds Media brings “community” back, supporting local heritage, music, and art through local radio and television, and staying highly visible in the communities we serve. It’s in nearly every car and truck on the road. The old ways of radio simply haven’t kept up with the incredible new opportunities available today. So we built a company around them.

Indigenous Partnerships

Preserving heritage on the air.

Our work with Indigenous tribes throughout the U.S. is designed to educate and preserve the rich histories, cultures, and languages of each tribe, heritage that is sadly facing extinction in many cases.

4Winds Media has brought together experienced, well-known personalities and executives to oversee daily operations and carry this mission forward.
