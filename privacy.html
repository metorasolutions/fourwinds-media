Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Four Winds Media collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information we collect

Information you provide (for example through our contact form):

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message content and topics of interest

Automatically collected:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website
How we use your information
  • Respond to your inquiries
  • Share information about our stations, content, and events
  • Send updates (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and keep the site secure
Information sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers who help operate the website (such as form processing and hosting)
  • Legal requirements and law enforcement requests
  • Business transfers (for example a merger or acquisition)
  • With your consent
Data security

We use SSL encryption and limit access to personal information. That said, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep inquiry records only as long as relevant, plus a reasonable period for business records. If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so within 30 days, except where the law requires longer retention.

Your privacy rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, to opt out of marketing communications, and to request a portable copy of your data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@4windsmedia.com.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies to keep it running (essential cookies) and to understand usage patterns (analytics cookies). You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended without them.
